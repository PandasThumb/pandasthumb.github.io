Zeugomantispa minuta

By Matt Young
August 3, 2026 11:00 MST

Photography Contest.

Al Denelsbeck, Runner-up.


Another optics problem! The submission was by design relatively low in resolution, so I asked for the original and cropped it so as to reveal more information. More below the fold. Hard to say, but it looks to me like the diffraction pattern of a hexagonal array, probably seen as an image in the “len” formed by the eye (a “len” is half a thin lens). I would have expected it to be rotated by 30°, though, and the colors in the secondary maxima do not look right. Maybe someone else can weigh in.
